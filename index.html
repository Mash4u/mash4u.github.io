About Me

Welcome to my academic website. I am Muhammad Abdullahi, a PhD researcher in Computational Mathematics at Central South University (CSU), Changsha, China. I am also an Assistant Lecturer in the Department of Mathematics at Sule Lamido University Kafin Hausa (SLUK), Nigeria.

My research focuses on numerical optimization, nonsmooth and nonconvex optimization, primal-dual splitting algorithms, monotone operator theory, derivative-free iterative methods, conjugate-gradient methods, image recovery and machine learning. I develop and analyse accelerated iterative algorithms, establish convergence properties (global convergence, convergence rates and computational complexity), and implement them in MATLAB and Python for large-scale benchmark problems.

I have published 26 journal articles in venues including Numerical Algorithms, Communications in Nonlinear Science and Numerical Simulation, IMA Journal of Applied Mathematics, International Journal of Computer Mathematics, AIMS Mathematics, and Scientific Reports.

Research Interests

Numerical Optimization Monotone Operator Theory Derivative-Free Methods Conjugate-Gradient Methods Image Recovery Machine Learning Nonsmooth & Nonconvex Optimization Primal-Dual Splitting Algorithms Large-Scale Nonlinear Equations Signal Processing

Key methodological interests include accelerated iterative algorithms, inertial and double-step-length methods, spectral conjugate-gradient projection methods, global and Q-linear convergence analysis, and high-performance computing for large-scale problems. Applications span image restoration, signal recovery, motion control, data-driven optimization and medical image analysis.
